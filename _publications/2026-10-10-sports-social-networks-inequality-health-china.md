title: "Socioeconomic Inequality in Access to Sports Social Networks and Their Association with Health in China"
collection: publications
category: economics
status: working
permalink: /publication/2026-sports-social-networks-inequality-health-china
date: 2026-10-10
authors: 'Longlong Duan, Leiyu Shi, <b>Kowkam Zhang</b>'
excerpt: 'Using the 2024 China National Sports and Society Survey (n = 8,428), we find that access to sports social networks (sports organizations, local informal groups, and online communities) is strongly stratified by socioeconomic status, with participation rising from 26.2% to 57.8% across education levels. Network participation is associated with better self-rated health, higher life satisfaction, and fewer depressive symptoms, and these associations are similar across SES groups, a pattern of unequal access but similar returns. A randomized vignette experiment shows that high fees and a 30-minute travel distance reduce willingness to exercise to a similar degree across SES groups. Reducing access barriers may therefore narrow health inequalities, although the cross-sectional design and residual confounding limit causal interpretation.'
