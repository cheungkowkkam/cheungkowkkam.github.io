I'm a PhD Student in Health System Research (Health Economic Emphasis) from [the Institute of Health Policy, Management & Evaluation, University of Toronto](https://ihpme.utoronto.ca/). My research interests lie in **health economics, labor economics, and environmental economics**, with a particular focus on primary healthcare, health inequality, health disparity, underserved populations, and technological innovation. To date, my work has been published in *Finance Research Letters* and *Labor Economics Review* (CSSCI-indexed), and I have three additional working papers in progress.

Previously, I was a full-time Research Associate from the [Johns Hopkins Primary Healthcare Policy Center](https://publichealth.jhu.edu/johns-hopkins-primary-care-policy-center). I had the honor of earning a Master of Health Science degree (Global Health Economics Track) from the [Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health](https://publichealth.jhu.edu/), where I met an advisor who may have changed the course of my life: *Prof. Leiyu Shi*. Prior to that, I earned my Bachelor’s degree in Economics from Sichuan University, where I ranked first in my major based on overall academic performance, and received the National Scholarship.

I have also maintained a long-standing interest in the translation and investment of medical technologies. As team leader, I won a **National Gold Award** in the Science and Technology Track of [the 2025 “Chuang Qingchun” China Youth Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition](https://cqc.yeeol.com/)[^1], and **National Silver Awards** in [the China International College Students’ Innovation Competition](https://www.pilcchina.org/)[^2] in both 2024 and 2025. Previously, I was part of teams that won **National Gold Awards** in the same competition in 2022 and 2023.

As a first-generation college student, I am deeply grateful to my parents, mentors, friends, and alma mater for helping me transition from a small county in Northeast China to world-leading academic institutions.

[^1]:The “Chuang Qingchun” China Youth Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition is a national-level competition jointly organized by the Central Committee of the Communist Youth League of China, the Ministry of Education, and other national agencies. It is regarded as one of the most prestigious youth innovation and entrepreneurship competitions in China.

[^2]:The China International College Students Innovation Competition, organized by the Ministry of Education, with a letter from the General Secretary and attendance from the Vice Premier, is known as the premier competition in Chinese higher education. The event spans over a hundred countries, thousands of universities, and tens millions of participants.
